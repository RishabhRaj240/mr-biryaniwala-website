01 / DUM PUKHT CRAFT

The Sacred Copper Handi & Dough Purdah

In Hyderabadi culinary lore, Dum Pukht translates literally to "breathe in its own aroma." We do not rush. The meats and aged rice cook in their own steam, trapped under a hand-kneaded whole-wheat dough ring.

STEP 01

Purdah Dough Seal

Every single portion is individually sealed with whole-wheat dough, locking every molecule of moisture, saffron, and clove inside the vessel until cracked open at your table.

STEP 02

Heavy Copper & Kalai

We cook exclusively in hand-hammered heavy copper handis lined with fresh tin (*kalai*), ensuring radiant, uniform heat distribution that never scorches the basmati grain.

STEP 03

Dying Red Embers

The sealed handi is placed over gentle ironbark coals with a shovelful of burning coals piled directly onto the lid, creating true top-and-bottom convection dum cooking.

02 / BOTANY & AROMA

24 Sacred Potli Spices

Every morning at 6:00 AM, our spice master ties twenty-four whole botanicals into muslin cloth potlis. These are submerged deep into the broth to impart aroma without harsh grit.

केसर

Kashmiri Mongra Saffron

Pampore, Kashmir

Highest grade whole red stigmas, hand-plucked and steeped in warm whole milk for deep golden hue and honeyed aroma.

पत्थर फूल

Dagad Phool (Stone Flower)

Western Ghats

Wild lichen harvested from mossy rock faces. Releases the signature earthy, forest aroma indispensable to authentic Hyderabadi masala.

शाही जीरा

Shahi Caraway (Royal Cumin)

Kabul Valley

Dark, crescent-shaped seeds with notes of anise and pine, distinct from ordinary cumin, providing royal digestive balance.

जावित्री

Javitri (Mace Blades)

Malabar Coast

The delicate crimson aril encasing the nutmeg seed, bringing subtle warm sweetness and lingering fragrance to the meat marinade.

चक्र फूल

Chakra Phool (Star Anise)

Southern Highlands

Eight-pointed star pods roasted lightly to extract licorice warmth that harmonizes the rich goat and lamb marrow.

मथानिया मिर्च

Mathania Red Chillies

Rajasthan

Sun-cured desert pods prized for brilliant vermilion color, smoky fruitiness, and rounded heat that warms without blistering.
